GAMING PLAYER ANALYTICS
DATA DICTIONARY
============================================

1. PlayerID
Unique identifier for each player.

2. Age
Age of the player.

3. Gender
Gender category of the player.

4. Location
Location/country associated with the player.

5. GameGenre
Genre of the game played by the player.

6. PlayTimeHours
Number of hours the player spends playing.

7. SessionsPerWeek
Number of gaming sessions per week.

8. PlayerLevel
Current level/progression of the player.

9. InGamePurchases
Indicates whether the player made an in-game purchase.
0 = No purchase
1 = Purchase

10. EngagementLevel
Player engagement category.
Possible values:
Low
Medium
High

11. GameDifficulty
Difficulty category of the game.
Possible values:
Easy
Medium
Hard
